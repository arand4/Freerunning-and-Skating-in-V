@NAME@ @VERSION@
Mirror's Edge parkour and Skate 3 skating in GTA V Legacy story mode.
An unofficial fan project, unaffiliated with EA, DICE, Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
It contains no Mirror's Edge, Skate 3 or GTA V code or data.

This product builds on:

Faith Runner (MIT License)
  Copyright (c) 2026 Tanner. https://github.com/tnrjns/faith-runner
  The movement (crates/faith_move) is used with changes for GTA's hollow, detailed buildings
  (patches/faith-runner-gta-changes.patch): a vault needs a top above the wall; ledge grabs and
  pull-ups need a clear way over the lip; a host can mark an obstacle to vault right across
  (World::vault_across, car bonnets); wallclimbs can step out round small overhangs
  (Tuning::overhang_step_out, off by default); springboards can launch off a step into a wall
  (Tuning::springboard_into_walls, off by default), keeping the run-in speed for the wallclimb,
  or off a step at a building's edge, such as a roof's parapet with at least 2 m of drop beyond it
  (Tuning::springboard_into_air, off by default).
  Also used: crates/faith_anim (Faith's first-person animation, body placement, camera and
  sound director), with one change: a faster ladder climb (Tuning::ladder_climb_speed) speeds up
  the arms by only its square root; and crates/me_assets (Mirror's Edge package readers), unchanged.
  Full licence text: parkour\LICENSE-faith-runner.txt; the changes: parkour\faith-runner-gta-changes.patch

SK8V (Apache License 2.0)
  Copyright 2026 becaring. https://github.com/becaring/SK8V
  Used: rust/svwc (GTA collision bound decoding, unchanged); rust/skatev-runtime/src/live.rs
  (copied, changed: glam instead of bevy_math, tests removed); host/src/physics_level.cpp and
  game_probe.cpp (ported to Rust in src/level.rs); host/src/pose_hook.cpp and the skeleton reading
  (ported to Rust in src/posehook.rs); rust/skatev-runtime/src/ped.rs's retarget method and
  bone map (rewritten for Faith's skeleton in src/retarget.rs). Those parts are in parkour\FaithRunnerV.asi (Faith Runner V). @NAME@ is not made or endorsed by
  SK8V's author.
  Real-time collision detection: credit to Sol4ra. The live GTA collision reader is adapted
  by SK8V from Sol4ra's LS-Skate-LiveCollision.
  Full licence text: parkour\LICENSE-SK8V.txt; SK8V's own notice: parkour\NOTICE-SK8V.txt

Mirror's Edge
  Faith's body, animations, textures and sounds come from the player's own Mirror's Edge
  install: the launcher copies the 16 package files they're in from it into the player's own
  GTA V folder, on the player's PC. None of it is included in this download.

Rust crates
  glam, windows (Microsoft), rodio, lewton and their dependencies keep their own MIT/Apache-2.0
  licences.

SK8V itself
  Not included. The launcher downloads becaring's own release from https://github.com/becaring/SK8V
  and runs his setup tools from it unchanged (tools\get-python.ps1, tools\prepare.py), showing their
  progress in its own window; or, for returning players, copies the two files from his update zip,
  as his release notes say. SK8V's name is used only to say what that card installs and where it comes from.

The launcher
  Rust, with tao and wry (Tauri Programme, Apache-2.0/MIT), ureq, zip, rfd, winreg, sha2, trash,
  serde and windows-sys, each under its own MIT and/or Apache-2.0 licence. The SK8V card shows
  SK8V's own media/sk8v.webp (Apache-2.0, becaring).
